The Laboratory

Where Gold Gets Nothing But The Truth.

Located in the heart of Saddar, the historic commercial center of Karachi, Dubai Gold Test Lab is an independent assay service built on one principle: unbiased, defensible results. We test bullion, jewellery, coins, scrap and industrial alloys using internationally recognised methods.

  • Independence Owned and run separately from any refiner, trader or retailer — no conflicts of interest.
  • Speed Express testing available; standard certificates issued within 24 hours.
  • Traceability Every report is reference-numbered, photo-documented and verifiable online.

The Process

From Drop-off to Certificate in Four Steps

No long queues, no opaque procedures. A clear, supervised chain of custody from the moment your item arrives until your signed certificate is in hand.

Chain of Custody

Sealed translucent bags, photo intake and a unique barcode follow your item at every stage.

Secure Deliverables

Certificates issuable as tamper-proof PDF, embossed paper, or both on request.

  1. 1

    Bring or Send

    Visit our lab at Aladdin House in Saddar, or arrange insured pickup from anywhere in Pakistan.

  2. 2

    Intake & Inspection

    Your item is weighed, photographed, sealed and booked into the laboratory management system in your presence.

  3. 3

    Testing & Analysis

    Master assayers run calibrated XRF, density or cupellation analysis with independent second-operator review.

  4. 4

    Report & Release

    You receive the signed certificate and your item back, fully recorded and released against your ID.
